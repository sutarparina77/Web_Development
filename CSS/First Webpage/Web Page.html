Nature encompasses everything in the physical world that isn't made by humans. It includes wildlife, weather, ecosystems, and natural landscapes like forests, oceans, and mountains. It is the life-support system of the Earth, providing the air we breathe, the water we drink, and the food we eat. The natural world is made up of interconnected systems, life forms, and physical phenomena. Nature underpins all life on Earth and provides fundamental services often referred to as "natural capital".
